Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026.

We value your privacy. We collect the personal information you choose to provide when you contact our team.

Information We Collect

When you submit the contact form or email us, we receive the information you choose to provide. This may include:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Company / organization
  • Role / profession
  • Type of collaboration
  • Message

How the Contact Form Works

When you submit the form, your details are sent securely to Web3Forms, which processes the inquiry and forwards a notification to our team. Web3Forms may store submissions and process technical information for delivery and spam prevention. See the Web3Forms Privacy Policy for details about its providers, storage and international processing. You can also contact us directly at contact@mbchmedia.com.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your inquiries
  • Explore potential collaborations or partnerships

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We use Web3Forms and our email provider to process and receive your inquiries. These providers and their service providers process information needed to operate the service. We may also disclose information when required by law.

Data Retention

We retain the inquiries we receive only as long as necessary to respond and explore the proposed collaboration. Web3Forms has its own submission retention settings. Its published policy states a maximum of three years, unless a shorter period applies or the data is deleted earlier. You may contact us to request deletion.

Your Rights

If you wish to access, correct, or delete your personal data, please contact us at contact@mbchmedia.com.

Cookies and Technical Information

This website does not use analytics or advertising cookies, tracking scripts, or local storage. Your browser and the hosting provider may process technical information needed to deliver the pages. When you submit a form, Web3Forms and its providers may process technical information for delivery, security and spam prevention.
